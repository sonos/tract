Bonsai Image, in your browser

Runs on your GPU through WebGPU with tract. Nothing leaves your machine.

Starting…

The first visit downloads the model (about 4.7 GB) and keeps it in your browser's storage for next time. Ternary Bonsai Image 4B by PrismML, Apache-2.0: a Qwen3 text encoder, a ternary Flux.2 Klein transformer and its VAE. The first image loads all three; later ones are quick.

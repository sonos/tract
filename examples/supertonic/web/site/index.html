Speak some text, in your browser

Supertone's Supertonic reads your text aloud with tract, on the CPU or on WebGPU. Your text is not uploaded. The model is fetched from Hugging Face the first time and kept in this browser.

Press Speak to hear the text.

The voice you hear is synthesized speech, made by a machine learning model; it is not a recording of a person. Model: Supertonic by Supertone Inc., used under the BigScience OpenRAIL-M license, whose use restrictions (Attachment A) apply to anything you make with it, including no impersonation and no deceptive use. The ONNX graphs run unchanged in tract, compiled to WebAssembly. Source code

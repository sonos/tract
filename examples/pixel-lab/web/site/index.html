Background replacement, in your browser

Person segmentation on your camera, on your GPU through WebGPU with tract. Nothing leaves your machine.

Starting…

The camera frames stay on the GPU; only the models come over the network, straight from Hugging Face at a pinned revision, and stay in your browser's cache: MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation (0.5 MB) and MODNet (26 MB), both under the Apache 2.0 license. The camera is composited at its own frame rate with the latest mask, so a slower model makes the mask lag, not the video; the mask latency runs from handing a frame to the model to its mask being ready to composite.

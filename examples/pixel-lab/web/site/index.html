The camera frames stay on the GPU; only the models come over the network, and they stay in your browser's cache. MediaPipe Selfie Segmentation, square or landscape (0.5 MB), and MODNet (26 MB) at 512 × 288, 448 × 256 and 384 × 224, come from Hugging Face at a pinned revision, under the Apache 2.0 license. RVM MobileNetV3 is the official v1.0.0 fp32 file (15 MB), GPL-3.0. The page downloads it from a pinned Hugging Face copy, because the release host does not let a web page read it. It reads the camera frame itself. Auto keeps the long side of the inner frame at 512, and a smaller camera is not shrunk. A fixed R factor replaces that, including while the camera is running. Changing it clears the model's memory. The composited picture uses the camera frame the mask was measured from, so a slower model holds that pair instead of painting the new picture with the old mask. The preview keeps running live. The mask latency runs from handing a frame to the model to its mask being ready to composite. “Smooth mask edges” softens the mask on its own. “Move the mask with the camera” shows the newest frame and moves the mask with the person, using one motion measured where the picture has real detail. Skin follows that motion. Only the outline is checked against the picture, so a flat colour does not show through a fringe or a hand that has closed, and the face is left whole. It holds the matched pair when the warp has no source frame. A one-pixel hole inside the mask is closed. Coverage the mask never had is not invented. Source code

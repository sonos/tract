tract in your browser

Neural networks running in your browser, on your GPU through WebGPU or on your CPU, with tract, compiled to WebAssembly. Nothing leaves your machine.

Stable Diffusion 1.5

Text to image, 512 × 512, with a prompt, a negative prompt and a choice of sampler.

Downloads 2.1 GB of fp16 weights from Hugging Face on the first visit. Needs WebGPU with shader-f16.

Background replacement

Person segmentation on your camera, with the background blurred or replaced by a colour, live.

Uses your camera. Downloads a 0.5 MB or 26 MB model from Hugging Face. Needs WebGPU.

Hey Snips

Wake word detection on your microphone, streaming, from raw samples to a score every 10 ms.

Uses your microphone. Runs on the CPU in WebAssembly, no WebGPU needed. 1.2 MB model.

Source code of the demos, in tract's examples.
